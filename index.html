A Letter For You

WRITTEN WITH A LITTLE COURAGE

Dear You,

Happy Birthday.

I know we're not what we used to be, and maybe that's exactly how life was supposed to unfold. Still, I couldn't let your birthday pass without wishing you something beautiful.

There was a time when your happiness meant everything to me. Maybe a part of me will always want good things for you.

I remember the smiles, the late conversations, the silly moments, the little arguments, and all those ordinary days that somehow became extraordinary because you were there.

Sometimes I wonder how two people can go from being so close to becoming strangers who simply remember each other.

But I've learned that not every person who leaves your life was a mistake. Some people leave behind memories, lessons, and a version of yourself that you wouldn't have discovered otherwise.

I don't want this letter to make you feel guilty, confused, or responsible for anything. I'm not asking for another chance. I'm not asking you to come back.

I just wanted you to know that once, you were genuinely important to me. And even though things changed, I can still appreciate what we had.

On your birthday, I hope you smile more. I hope you meet people who appreciate your heart. I hope you achieve the things you've been dreaming about. And most importantly, I hope you're happy.

Maybe someday we'll both look back at our story without sadness. Just a quiet smile... because once upon a time, we meant something to each other.

Happy Birthday.
Take care of yourself. ♡
